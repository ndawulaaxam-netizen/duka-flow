2. The service

DukaFlow helps shopkeepers record sales, stock, expenses and profits; view daily and weekly reports; scan barcodes; and keep an automatic cloud backup. It is built to work offline and to sync when internet is available.

3. Completely free for shopkeepers

DukaFlow is 100% free for shopkeepers. No subscriptions, no hidden charges, no in-app purchases, no trial periods. Forever.

4. How DukaFlow makes money (the honest part)

With your consent, we combine the records of many shops into aggregated, anonymized market summaries (for example: "how many units of a product sold in a region this week"). We sell these summaries to FMCG manufacturers, distributors and financial institutions so that shops like yours get better stock and service.

What we will NEVER do:

  • Never sell or share your individual shop records with anyone.
  • Never publish or reveal your shop name, owner name, phone number or location in any report.
  • Never see or store your PIN in readable form (it is stored as an encrypted hash).
  • Never build a report small enough to identify a single shop.

5. Your account and your PIN

  • You create a 4-digit PIN when registering. Keep it secret. Anyone with your phone number and PIN can open your shop records in the app.
  • We cannot recover or reset your PIN. We only store an encrypted fingerprint of it. If you forget it, you may need to register a new shop profile.
  • Logging out does not delete your records from your phone. "Delete Data on This Phone" erases the local copy; your cloud backup stays safe until you ask us to remove it.

6. Your data and your rights

  • You own your shop data. We only hold it for you.
  • Records are stored on your device and backed up to our secure cloud.
  • You can see all your data inside the app at any time.
  • You can erase the phone copy in Settings, and you can email or WhatsApp us to delete your cloud backup and close your account. We will complete deletion within 14 days.

7. Consent to aggregated analytics

By ticking the consent box when registering, you allow us to include your records in aggregated, anonymized market insights (Section 4). You may withdraw this consent at any time by contacting us — and DukaFlow will still remain free for you.

8. Offline first — our promise and its limits

DukaFlow is built offline-first and syncs automatically when internet returns. However, no software or network is perfect. For matters that are legally or financially critical (tax, loans, contracts), keep your own independent records. We do not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free service.
