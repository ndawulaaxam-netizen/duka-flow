DukaFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

1. What we collect

When you register, we collect your shop name, owner name, phone number and location. When you use the app, we collect the sales, expenses and stock records you enter.

2. How we use it

Your records power your personal business reports (profit, stock levels, sales history) and your automatic cloud backup.

3. What we share

We may combine the records of many shops after removing all names, phone numbers and shop identities. Only these combined, anonymous market trends are shared with partners such as manufacturers and distributors, to help them supply shops better. Your individual shop data is never shared in a way that identifies you or your shop.

4. Data security

Data is stored on secure cloud servers and works offline on your phone until a connection is available. Your cloud re-login is protected by your secret PIN.

5. Your choices

You can delete the data on your phone anytime in Settings, or ask us to delete your cloud backup by contacting us below.
