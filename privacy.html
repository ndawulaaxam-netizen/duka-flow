DukaFlow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Written in plain English on purpose. The short version: your records belong to you, we never sell your individual shop data, and we only earn money from combined, anonymized market summaries.

1. Who we are

DukaFlow is a free shop-assistant app built in Uganda by Acksam Ndawula.
Email: axamndawula@gmail.com • Phone/WhatsApp: +256 742 679 856

2. What we collect

  • Shop profile: shop name, owner name, phone number, location — the details you type at registration.
  • Your records: the sales, stock, expenses, products, barcodes and prices you enter.
  • Your PIN: stored ONLY as an encrypted fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash). We can never read your PIN, and we cannot reset it.
  • Technical basics: the app stores data on your phone (localStorage) so it works offline, and keeps a copy in our secure cloud (Supabase) as your backup.

We do not use advertising cookies and we do not track you across other websites.

3. The shared product index (barcode network)

When you add a product with a barcode, we add a small public entry containing ONLY: barcode number, product name, category and typical price. This lets any other shop scan that barcode and get the product name instantly.

These entries contain no shop name, no phone number, no sales counts and no owner identity.

4. How we use your data

  • To run the app: backup, restore, multi-device refresh, reports and stock alerts.
  • With your consent, to build aggregated, anonymized market summaries (for example: total units of a product sold per region per week). These summaries are sold to FMCG manufacturers, distributors and financial institutions — this is how DukaFlow stays free for you.

What we will NEVER do:

  • Never sell or share your individual shop records.
  • Never reveal your shop name, owner, phone or location in any report.
  • Never build a report small enough to identify a single shop.
  • Never store your PIN in readable form.

5. Where your data lives

On your phone (so the app works with no internet) and in our secure cloud database (Supabase) over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our cloud provider is contractually bound to keep data secure.

6. How long we keep it

While your shop account exists. If you ask us to close your account and delete your cloud backup, we complete deletion within 14 days. Aggregated summaries created before deletion remain, but they can never be traced back to you.

7. Your rights

  • See your data in the app at any time.
  • Correct it by editing in the app.
  • Delete the phone copy in Settings, and request cloud deletion by email or WhatsApp.
  • Withdraw your analytics consent at any time — DukaFlow stays free for you.
  • Complain to the Uganda Personal Data Protection Office if you believe we mishandled your data.

8. Security

Encrypted connections everywhere, hashed PINs, and a private key per shop. You are responsible for keeping your PIN secret — anyone with your phone number and PIN can open your records in the app.

9. Children

DukaFlow is a business tool intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and show a notice in the app for major changes. Continued use means acceptance.
